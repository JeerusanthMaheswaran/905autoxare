FAQ

Good to Know

Do you deliver the equipment?

Yes. Our own flatbeds deliver to job sites and pick up when you're done. Include your site address when you book and we'll quote delivery too.

Can I rent a machine with an operator?

Yes. Choose "Rental with Operator" on the booking form and tell us about the job.

How quickly will you reply?

Booking requests come straight to our WhatsApp, and we reply as fast as we can. For anything urgent, call our 24/7 dispatch at 437-770-5440.

Do you repair equipment you didn't rent out?

Yes. We repair and service heavy equipment of all kinds, in our shop or on site. Send us the make, model and the problem on WhatsApp.
